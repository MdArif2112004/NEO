Everyone sees the headlines about the forty five crore Don 3 disaster, but the real conspiracy is how the Bollywood machine is quietly trying to execute Ranveer Singh's career. Farhan Akhtar didn't just complain about a broken contract. He went straight to the Directors' Association, who then handed a loaded gun to FWICE, the most powerful workers union in the industry. Excel Entertainment claims they lost forty five crore on pre production and promos because Ranveer walked out. But look at Ranveer's side of the table. Years of development, allegedly no locked script, and zero advance payment for his time. That is not just some PR spin about creative differences, that is a massive financial standoff. But instead of settling it in a boardroom, the establishment is closing ranks. FWICE has now issued a non cooperation directive against Ranveer. They are legally covering their tracks by claiming it is not an official ban and that members can simply choose not to work with him. But let us be absolutely real. When the union tells technicians, camera crews, and makeup artists to step back, it is functionally devastating. Even veteran filmmaker Ram Gopal Varma called them out, slamming FWICE as a kangaroo court and a massive PR disaster. Ranveer Singh built his empire from the ground up without a massive film family protecting him. Now, for walking away from a project that allegedly wasn't even creatively ready, they are weaponizing the industry's own workers to make a brutal example out of him. If this standoff isn't resolved immediately, we are watching the orchestrated, systematic takedown of a superstar.
