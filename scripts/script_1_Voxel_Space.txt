You can explore entire galaxies in under 10 seconds. 
Voxel Space is a mind-blowing project that renders 3D spaces using voxels, creating stunning visuals. 
It's like Minecraft, but instead of blocks, it uses tiny 3D pixels to generate entire worlds. 
The creator has even made it open-source, so anyone can try it out. 
But here's the question: what would you create if you had the power to generate entire universes in seconds?
